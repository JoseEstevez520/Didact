**Non-text failures found.** `border` (both themes) and `ring` (light theme) fall well under 3:1 against `background`: `border` is an intentionally subtle hairline (`docs/visual-language.md`, "Restraint": "hairline borders") that was never designed to meet the 3:1 UI-boundary threshold, and the dark theme's `border`/`input` additionally carry alpha transparency (10%/15%) which erodes contrast further once composited over the near-black background. `ring` (the focus indicator) is the more consequential failure in light mode: WCAG 2.4.7/1.4.11 care about focus visibility, and a solo focus ring at 2.6:1 in light mode is a real, actionable gap — separate from the fact that `docs/visual-language.md` also specifies a ring-colored *border* alongside the ring itself as the actual focus treatment, which this audit does not evaluate as a combined effect. This is reported honestly as an open item rather than silently patched here: this task's scope was to auto-adjust TEXT pairs that fail 4.5:1 (none did), not to redesign the non-text tokens, which is a broader visual-design call. Tracked as a follow-up.
